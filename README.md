## Overview

Spooky Mod adds a Halloween-themed content expansion to Terraria featuring a new underground Spooky biome inhabited by unique enemies, several original boss encounters including pre-hardmode and hardmode fights, over 100 unique items themed around pumpkins, ghosts, and the macabre, and a complete atmospheric overhaul of the October seasonal event.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **Spooky Mod** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.
